- You may **cancel your subscription at any time** from Settings → Subscription, or by contacting

- **Case-by-case exceptions.** We may, at our discretion, consider refund requests in exceptional
  circumstances (for example, an accidental duplicate charge, a verified technical failure on our

- Statutory consumer rights in your jurisdiction (for example EU withdrawal rights, where
  applicable) are not affected by this policy.

## 2. One-Time Purchases

- Where a one-time purchase is offered, it is refundable within 14 days of the

- To qualify, your usage during that period must remain within a reasonable evaluation threshold
  indicated on the pricing page or in the product at the time of purchase.
- Refund requests made after that window, or where usage exceeded the threshold, are not eligible
  except where required by law.

## 3. Free Trial

- The free trial requires no payment details and you are **never charged automatically** when it
  ends; your account simply returns to the Free plan.
- Because the free trial involves no charge, this policy's refund provisions apply to **paid
  subscriptions and one-time purchases only**, not to the free trial.

## 4. Referral and Affiliate Rewards

- Referral credits have no cash value and are not refundable.
- If a refunded purchase generated an affiliate commission, that commission may be reversed.

## 5. How to Request a Refund

1. Email `support@myautoeco.com` from the address associated with your account.
2. Include: your account email, the date and amount of the charge, the plan purchased, and a brief
   reason for the request.
3. We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the
   original payment method via our payment processor; processing time depends on your bank or card
   issuer.
