## 2. Description of the Service

AutoEco is a financial record-keeping and calculation tool for vehicle ownership. The Service
allows you to:

- Record the vehicles you own or are responsible for;

Figures that are projections or estimates are labeled as such throughout the Service. The

not forecasts of future market conditions.
